About me

I'm Diana, a Slovak photojournalist, writer and documentary filmmaker.

I document the daily lives of women and the issues they face, and give a voice to topics from taboos to women's rights and local customs.

My assignments have taken me to DR Congo, Senegal and Ethiopia. I came to Cuba for two months in 2015 and have lived in Havana ever since.
