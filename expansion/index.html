In practice

Four real examples. Step by step, all at once, revitalization, CEE growth.

Example 1 · Step by step

Edimo: 2 → 7 markets. And 6 → 14 business units.

Edimo sells solar power for homes and companies: an online shop, installations and wholesale. It was at home in Slovakia and Czechia. This is how it went from 2 countries to 7, one at a time.

1

Austria

Online shop + installations

Why first: close to home, attractive margins, mature customers, and competitors we could match on price.

What we did: built a simple page that collects enquiries for installations. Launched the ads and tuned them week by week: dozens to hundreds of ad versions for different customer groups, their needs and their pains. Translated the online shop and adapted the product range for Austria.

First enquiries: within weeks.

2

Switzerland

Installations only

A much harder market: several languages, and different subsidy rules in every canton. This is where we started to use AI intensively to adapt the campaigns to each language.

3

Romania

Online shop, then wholesale

We entered with the online shop only. Wholesale followed.

4

Hungary

Online shop

We entered with the online shop.

5

Germany

Installations, online shop in preparation

We launched installations and prepared the online shop, built on the foundations from Austria.

Example 2 · All at once

GreenTech B2B e-commerce: 4 → 27 countries. In one go.

A client under NDA. We cloned the online shop and the section for business customers into 13 languages, and started the campaigns in 27 European countries at the same time.

How we decide how much each country gets: 1. How big is the market? 2. How many of the right people can we reach there? 3. What does one new customer cost there? 4. That sets the budget per country, and the weekly numbers move it.
