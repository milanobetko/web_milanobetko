How it works

Start with a Deep Dive. Continue with sparring.

Phase 1 / Weeks 1 to 4 / Optional start

Deep Dive: a fresh look at your entire setup, challenged.

  1. Where are we now?

    Diagnosis.

    Interviews with you, the CEO and the sales team. Full marketing funnel and sales pipeline. A structured review of the whole setup: positioning, funnel, channels, content, tools and data, budgets and processes.

  2. Where do we want to go?

    Strategy.

    Deep work on the biggest gaps and levers, with the latest best practices for short- and long-term upside. Brand and marketing strategy, co-created with you in a workshop.

  3. How do we get there?

    Plan.

    A written, honest assessment and a 2027 plan: what works, the biggest gaps and levers, and the initiatives I would prioritise and why. Presented and discussed as a workshop.

Phase 2 / 3 to 6 months / Prepaid monthly

Sparring: active and proactive, not on-demand Q&A.

  1. Send up to 2 topics a week.

    A plan, a budget, a brief, a campaign concept, an agency proposal or a hire.

  2. Get a written answer within 48 hours.

    Challenged from the brand, sales and P&L side.

  3. Talk it through weekly or bi-weekly.

    A 90-minute call, depending on your needs. I bring my own observations and ideas, not only answers to your topics.

Sparring only. I challenge and help you decide. You keep the lead, the team and the credit. Nothing is built around me, so nothing breaks when the engagement ends.

How I work

Three pillars. Everything in writing.

  1. Know-how transfer and people development.

    I pass on the best of what I have learned, so that you feel a real step change in your ability to deliver and lead, including ways of thinking and how to find best practices and inspiration.

  2. Focus on strengths.

    I start from what already works and keep building on it, improving through iteration and testing rather than launching one new initiative after another.

  3. Best practices and evidence-based judgment.

    I bring in the latest best practices and judge what each channel can contribute to business growth, based on client-side experience and hundreds of client cases at top media agency networks.

Everything in writing. The assessment and every review land as written documents. The value stays in your company, not in my head.
